QR Code Generator

Generate QR codes from any text or link.

A free QR code generator whose codes really are permanent

Most “free” QR generators hand you a dynamic QR code: the image points at their short link, which redirects to yours. If that company shuts the link down, raises its price, or goes out of business, every code you printed stops working.

This tool makes static QR codes instead. Your link or text is encoded directly into the black-and-white pattern, so the code is self-contained. There is no redirect, no server, no account, and no scan limit — which is why it cannot expire, and why it will still scan in ten years on a business card, a poster, a menu or a product label.

How to use it

  • Paste a URL or any text and press Generate QR Code.
  • Download the PNG and print it at any size — the pattern is resolution-independent.
  • Test the printed code before a large print run, and leave a quiet margin around it.

The trade-off of a static code: because the destination is baked in, you cannot change where it points afterwards, and you get no scan analytics. Generation happens entirely in your browser — the text you encode is never sent anywhere.
