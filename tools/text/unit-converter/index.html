📏 Unit Converter

Convert between common and scientific units in real time.

Convert every unit in a category at once

Type a value into any box and every other unit in that group updates immediately — you do not pick a “from” and a “to” unit. That makes it quick to sanity-check an answer against several units at the same time.

What is covered

  • Length — mm, cm, m, km, inches, feet, yards, miles, micrometres, nanometres, nautical miles, astronomical units.
  • Mass — mg, g, kg, tonnes, pounds, ounces, stone, slugs.
  • Temperature — Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin, Rankine.
  • Volume & area — mL, L, m³, cubic inches and feet, US and UK gallons, barrels; mm² to square miles, hectares and acres.
  • Speed, time, energy, pressure — m/s, km/h, mph, knots, the speed of light; nanoseconds to centuries; joules, calories, kWh, electronvolts, BTU; pascals, bar, atmospheres, psi, torr.

Conversion factors are exact where an exact definition exists (an inch is exactly 25.4 mm, a pound exactly 0.45359237 kg). Everything is computed locally — the converter works with no internet connection at all.
