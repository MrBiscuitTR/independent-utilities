Text Art Clipboard

Quickly find and copy text emoticons, symbols, and art — search by name or character.

Find the emoticon, copy it, move on

A searchable clipboard of text emoticons (kaomoji), symbols and small ASCII art. Search by what it is called (“shrug”, “table flip”, “bear”), by a tag (“angry”, “cute”, “arrow”) or by typing part of the characters themselves. Click any tile to copy it.

What is in here

  • Faces & emotions — shrug, Lenny face, disapproval, crying, hug, facepalm, sleeping.
  • Symbols — arrows, checkmarks and crosses, stars, hearts, music notes, degree and currency-adjacent marks, dashes and ellipses.
  • Dividers — lines and box-drawing characters for separating sections in a README, a bio or a chat message.
  • ASCII art — small multi-line pieces that keep their shape in a monospaced font.

All of it is plain Unicode, so it pastes into Discord, Twitch, GitHub, Reddit, Word or a terminal without an image. Use the Max length filter when you have a character limit to respect. The list is local to the page — nothing is requested from anywhere.
